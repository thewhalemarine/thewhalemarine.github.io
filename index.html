ABOUT THE WHALE MARINE

Supplying Ships. Supporting Global Trade.

THE WHALE MARINE is a professional Ship Chandler and Marine Supplier based in Port Said, Egypt.

We provide ship provisions, bonded stores, deck and engine stores, safety supplies, cabin stores and general marine supplies to vessels calling at Port Said, the Suez Canal and Egyptian ports.

Our team is committed to reliable service, competitive prices and timely delivery to vessels.
